The pass that closes `dependent_classlikes` over dependees in `Populator::populateCodebase()` is quadratic for hub classes (`ArrayAccess`, `Throwable`, `Countable`, ...): `+=` on a typed array property computes the sum into a temporary and assigns it back, so every merge copies the whole left array. The merge now goes into a local array that is assigned to the property once per class.

On a private big Laravel app (about 18k classes, PHP 8.5, macOS arm64, 8 threads) the pass went from about 1.7 s to about 0.01 s per run. The resulting `dependent_classlikes` of every class were identical (`===`) to the old code's on that app, laravel-framework, drupal and mediawiki.
